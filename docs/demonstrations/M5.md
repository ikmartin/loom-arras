# M5. Digests

## Book says

`loom digest extract Man12` on the Manolache source in the relloc quilt; `\cite[Theorem 4.1]{Man12}` resolves; a bundle of a relloc lemma compiles with Manolache's theorem in it.

The relloc paper cites Manolache under the citekey `manolache_VirtualPullbacks2012` and names Theorems 4.3 and 4.8, Propositions 2.33 and 3.11, Remark 2.3, and Construction 3.13, so those are the postnotes demonstrated; the digest prefix is the citekey's slug `manolacheVirtualPullbacks2012` (DR-45).

The extraction reads the Manolache source, compiles it in a scratch directory for its `.aux`, and writes `refs/manolache_VirtualPullbacks2012.tex`:

```

Environment setting is not declared in this quilt; add \newtheorem{setting}[theorem]{Setting}
```

Every result of the paper is an external node with the paper's own number as its locator (`{\cite[Theorem 4.3, p.~26]{manolache_VirtualPullbacks2012}}`), its statement verbatim with the seven simple macros expanded, its labels prefixed, and a `\uses` line naming the results its dropped proof referred to (33 in all). The source directive is the DOI from the bibliography, since the entry is the journal version.

Two author-side steps followed, both of the kind the book prescribes: three `% !LOOM environment:` directives in the relloc master for the environments Manolache uses that relloc does not declare, and the four required packages appended to `preamble.tex`. Re-extracted after that, the digest lints clean, `requires:` is empty, and the quilt's lint is `3 errors, 5 warnings, 23 infos` (the three errors are relloc's own dangling link and two unattached proofs from M4).

Every Manolache postnote in the paper resolves to a digest node:

## Deviations recorded

- DR-66: a digest node's own locator title cites the paper it digests; that citation is neither a postnote edge nor an unmatched postnote.
- DR-67: results the paper leaves unnumbered (`\newtheorem*`) get ids `<slug>-<abbrev>-star-<n>` and the locator `<Name> (unnumbered)`.
- DR-68: the emulator always runs, resynchronised at every result the `.aux` numbers, because an `.aux` numbers only labelled results; `numbering: emulated` is written only when no `.aux` could be produced.
- DR-69: a display name declared twice in the quilt (relloc's `thm` and `thm*`) maps to the numbered environment.
- Discovery: `requires:` is written net of the packages the quilt's default master already loads (8.5.6), so after the author adds the packages the directive disappears on re-extraction; the lint check `loom:missing-package` is what catches a later change of preamble.
- Discovery: the extractor keeps the paper's own labels as prefixed aliases on a line of their own inside the node, so a `\ref` in another digest node or in the overview reaches the result under either name.
- Discovery: the digest's `source:` falls back to the bibliography's DOI and then to `local:<file>` when there is no eprint.
