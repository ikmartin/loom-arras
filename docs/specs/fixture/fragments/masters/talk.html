1 Widgets

The definition

Definition 1 (Gadget)

A gadget is a widget whose involution has no fixed point.

The lemma

Lemma 3 (Orbits)

Every orbit of a widget has one or two points; compare (eq:fix) in Definition Widget.

Proof

The orbit of \(x\) is \(\{x, \sigma x\}\) because

\[\tag{2}\sigma(\sigma x) = x\]

by (eq:prose).
