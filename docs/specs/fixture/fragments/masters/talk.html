Widgets, in twenty minutes

1 Widgets

The definition

Definition 2.4 (Gadget)

A gadget is a widget whose involution has no fixed point.

The lemma

Lemma 1.2 (Orbits)

Every orbit of a widget has one or two points; compare (2) in Definition 1.1.

Proof

The orbit of \(x\) is \(\{x, \sigma x\}\) because

\[\tag{3}\sigma(\sigma x) = x\]

by (1).
