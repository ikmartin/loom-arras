Overview

Kresch constructs Chow groups for Artin stacks. This digest holds the standing assumptions and the one theorem the synthetic paper cites.

Setup

Theorem ([e]Standing assumptions, Section 1Kre99)

All stacks are algebraic and of finite type over a field.

Cycle groups

Theorem ([e]Theorem 2.1, p. 4Kre99)

For every Artin stack \(X\) the group \(A_*(X)\) is well defined and \(\Hom(A_*(X), \mathbb{Q})\) is functorial.
