# 0.31 — Your Changes leads with the mathematics

## Context

Review › Your Changes listed every edit to an open file since the person's look (`looks.py`, `pending.in_place`), whoever made it, since loom cannot tell an agent's edit from the person's. In the quant quilt on 2026-11-12 that meant the author was asked to keep their own homework answers, a file new since the look arrived as one card holding the whole file, and the `.aux` and `.log` that their editor writes beside `hull_ch1.tex` were changes too. The cards were broken as well: `TexProse.svelte` turned `$…$` into math with a pattern that ignored `\$`, so `\$100 … $100 - 62$` paired wrongly, sentences were typeset as math that cannot wrap, and the card ran far off to the right.

The author's reading: a person need not review their own changes, and the review the node system exists for is whether the mathematics still holds. That matters to a researcher or an editor and not to a Learner.

## Decisions

The author chose, on 2026-11-12, "mathematics first".

1. **`[review] track_edits`, off by default.** Off, there are no in-place changes, `loom serve` takes no look, the manifest carries no `changes_since`, and annotations are read against the text now. Suggested edits, acceptance, and Keep and Discard on suggestions are unchanged. On, everything is as plan 0.30 built it: for a quilt whose agents edit open files in place.
2. **Turning it on in Settings takes the look then**, so tracking starts from that moment and not from a look taken long ago. Turned on by hand in `config.toml`, `loom serve` takes a first look if there is none.
3. **A look never holds LaTeX's auxiliary files**, on or off: the list the Files tree hides (`arras/src/lib/shell/aux.ts`), kept beside it in loom as `files.LATEX_AUX`. A look taken before they were left out does not report them as gone.
4. **Your Changes, in order:** the blocks waiting to be accepted, then the changes to keep (suggested edits, and in-place edits where tracked), then To do. The name stays. The column's line reads "mathematics to accept, changes to keep".
5. **Cards render prose safely and stay in their column.** TexProse uses `math/delimit.ts`, which leaves `\$` a dollar sign and reads `$$…$$` as a display; a card's words scroll within it, at most 14em high.
6. **Agents are told what holds under both settings.** The orientation says an edit in place is seen nowhere but as a changed accepted block unless the quilt tracks edits, so a change to the author's text is proposed with `loom suggest`.

## The phase

One phase, on the branch `your-changes-math-first`.

- Loom: `scan/quilt.py` (the key, a warning for a value that is not a boolean), `cli/quilt.py` (the template), `config_edit.py` (editable, checked, published), `pending.look_texts` (None when off), `render/pending.py` (`changes_since`), `render/serve.py` (`first_look`), `render/api.py` (`settings-save` takes the look; `change-keep` `all` takes a first look only when on), `cli/changes_cmd.py`, `files.py` and `looks.py` (auxiliary files), `assets/ai/orientation.md`.
- Viewer: `routes/review/+page.svelte`, `review/sections.ts`, `math/TexProse.svelte`, `review/ChangeCard.svelte`, `shell/settings/catalog.ts` and `Agents.svelte` (Review edits made in place), `components/HelpDot.svelte`, `shell/access.ts`, `manifest/types.ts`.
- Docs: `model.md` (the look, Review, Settings), `agents.md`, `invariants.md` (undo's rule, a dated line), `principles.md` P6, `specs/manifest.md` (§1 `config`, `changes`, `changes_since`), `specs/write-api.md` (`change-keep`, `settings-save`).
- Tests: the synthetic quilt tracks edits, so the fixture and the served write suites keep their in-place edits; loom's tests of in-place edits turn tracking on with `_quilts.track_edits`.

*Accept:* on a scratch copy of the quant quilt with the default, Your Changes holds only the open suggested edits and the blocks to accept, and no look holds an auxiliary file; turning Review edits made in place on in Settings takes a look, and an edit then shows as a card in which `\$100` reads as a dollar amount and which stays inside the page; `scripts/verify full` passes.

## Implementation

### 0.31 · Your Changes leads with the mathematics (2026-11-12)

Built as the phase says, in d6104c8d, 75ad45a6, 24dccfd5 and ca750717, with the viewer vendored in 98ccd6a1 and a58d2f97. Checked by hand on a copy of the quant quilt, light and dark: with the default, Your Changes held the two open suggested edits and the blocks to accept, and the published manifest no look; turning Review edits made in place on in Settings wrote `[review] track_edits = true` and took a look of hull_ch1.tex, hwk1.tex and hwk2.tex without their `.aux` and `.log`; an edit to hwk1.tex then waited as a card reading "you post $62 and they post $38", the fraction typeset, inside the page. `scripts/verify full` passes on a58d2f97 plus this entry: 2541 unit and 44 TeX tests, and the viewer's 388 static, 76 write and 49 reading suites.

What differs from the plan, or was found on the way:

- The quant quilt's suggested edits insert whole problems, so the escaped dollar was not the only fault in their cards: TexProse handed `\begin{enumerate}` and `\begin{proof}` to MathJax, which typesets any environment and drew each as an error. A card now shows new words that hold an environment or a paragraph break as the publisher made them (`new_html`, published since 0.30 and unused until now), and TexProse leaves an environment that is not mathematics as text.
- Keep and Discard were offered only on a ready change, so a suggested edit the text already reads as (`taken`) could be recorded kept only with `loom changes --keep`. They now act on it, and its card says that Keep records it.
- A look taken before auxiliary files were left out still names them; they are skipped when it is read, so they do not come back as files gone.
- A `track_edits` that is not a boolean is off, with a warning, rather than read as true.

### After 0.31 · Keep never writes a suggestion twice, and a suggestion follows its block (2026-11-13)

In quant an agent filed 29 suggested edits on hwk1's seven proofs, and one Keep made all 29 unplaced. The Keep was on `e-2026-11-12-n7hcm7`, an older suggestion inserting the whole homework body after `\maketitle`, which had been written in seven times on 2026-11-12 (the take-twice bug, fixed in 1ac44c5e) without ever being recorded kept; the author had since worked inside that body, so it no longer read as taken, and Keep wrote it once more. hwk1 then held fourteen proofs, the pasted copy first, and the agent's suggestions, which name their blocks by place (`#proof:1` to `#proof:7`), now named the pasted copies, where the words they quote do not stand. Fixed:

- A suggestion of twenty words or more reads as taken, edited since, when nine in ten of its words stand in its block in order with others between (`records/edits.py`, `taken_how`); keeping it records it kept and writes nothing. A shorter text must stand as suggested, and a replacement whose old words still stand is never taken.
- A suggestion goes with the block it was written against (`home`): where its key no longer holds that text and exactly one other block of the same file does, it is placed, shown and kept there.

On a copy of quant as it was before that Keep, `n7hcm7` reads as taken, Keep writes nothing, and the 29 stay ready; on a copy as it is now, all 29 are placed on the author's own proofs, `#proof:8` to `#proof:14`. The pasted body itself is the author's to take out, by restoring the version kept before that Keep (`hwk1-apply-9`).
