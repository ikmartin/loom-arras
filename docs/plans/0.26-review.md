# 0.26 — Review in four

## Context

Plan 0.25 made every section of Review act. The author then asked how many of its eight panels overlap, and what a first-time user would make of them. Three overlaps account for most of it: Changes and Needs review are two queues for one edit; Incoming, Suggestions and Library's citation suggestions are all proposals, each with its own verbs; Sessions and History are both records. Mockups on the live viewer showed Review as four sections, and the author asked for this plan on 2026-11-04.

| Section | Holds | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Your text | Changes + Needs review, each block listed once with every reason it waits | `/review` |
| Proposed to you | Incoming + Suggestions + citation suggestions + proposed results | `/review/proposed` |
| Problems | as now | `/review/problems` |
| Record | History + Sessions, as tabs: Versions, Sessions, Everything recorded | `/review/record…` |

Library leaves Review for a page of its own under Docs, `/library`.

**The decisions, approved with this plan:**

1. **Your text lists every edit since the look, with Keep and Undo.** Loom can't tell who made an edit, and catching an agent's is the point of the look.
2. **Accept also keeps.** Accepting a block means the person has seen its edit, so the edit stops being listed, and one press settles both reasons. The reverse doesn't hold: Keep says nothing about the mathematics (P15), so a kept block still waits to be accepted.
3. **Take it on a suggestion writes at once**, keeping a version first. It is one decision with nothing to batch. An agent's copy still ends in its one Apply.
4. **What is taken in stays listed as an edit in Your text**, so Undo is the way back from a taken suggestion. Accepting or keeping it clears it.
5. **Leave it on a suggestion records it as resolved, not applied**, with Undo in the bar. Dismiss stays for prose suggestions, where it means the thing should not have been raised.
6. **Citations say Take it and Leave it too**, in the card and in the annotation box beside the text, which removes "accept" as a word for two different acts (K7). A citation taken becomes a reference note and writes no text, and the page says so.
7. **Proposed results keep Verify, Edit and Discard.** Verifying claims the copy matches the paper, which "take" would blur (P15). Each work with results gets a card that opens the queue.
8. **The command line and write API keep `apply` and `--applied`**, since that is what the record holds. Only the viewer's button changes, a small departure from K7.
9. **Suggestions on an agent's copy are neither cards nor counted.** They come in with the copy. The copy's card says how many there are and folds them.
10. **Never accepted stays uncounted, in a fold** at the foot of Your text, as does Accepted. Rows keep their anchors, so "See this block in Review" still lands.

**What stays:** only the author accepts, takes in, applies or restores (P15, K5), and an agent is refused each; loom writes the author's file only on the author's act, keeping the old text first (P7); the viewer names no loom command.

## How this plan is carried out

One master document, this one. Each phase starts in plan mode: the code is explored, the concrete changes are proposed, and the author approves them. When a phase is built and `scripts/verify full` passes, what was built is appended to the **Implementation** section. Arras is re-vendored into loom with each phase; a phase that changes the manifest regenerates the fixtures (`docs/specs/tools/refresh-fixture.sh`).

## Phases

### 0.26.1 · Proposed to you

One page, `/review/proposed`, for everything someone proposes to the person. Each proposal is a card, in order: a pull, agent copies, suggestions with text and citations newest first, then a card for each work with proposed results. A card opens its own review in the page at its own address (`?copy=`, `?pull`, `?work=`). Prose suggestions follow in a fold, uncounted. Incoming and Suggestions go; Library loses its queue and its citation list. No loom behaviour changes.

### 0.26.2 · Your text

- **Loom:**
  - `changes[]` gains `blocks`, the keys each diff group touches, from the changed lines against `nodes[].file` and `src`, and `documents`, from `documents_reaching`.
  - `changes-keep` and `changes-undo` take `hunks`, a list, so a block with two groups is one act; `loom changes --hunk` may repeat.
  - Accepting keeps every group touching only blocks just accepted, with one version kept.
  - Specs and fixtures follow.
- **Arras:**
  - `lib/review/yourText.ts` joins `changes` with `unresolved`.
  - `routes/review/+page.svelte` is rewritten: a document picker with counts, then one row per block with its tags, its own diff groups and Keep, Undo… and Accept.
  - Then come groups taken in from each source, text outside any block, Other files with no Accept, and the two folds.
  - `DiffView` gains a filter to named groups, and `/review/changes` goes.
- **Rule for a group spanning two blocks:** it is drawn under the first, and the second says "edited with First proof".

### 0.26.3 · Record, and Library under Docs

- `routes/review/record/` gets a layout with the heading and tabs, and pages for Versions, Sessions and Everything recorded, split from the History page. `HistoryMenu` links follow.
- The Library page moves to `/library` unchanged. The shelf's link becomes "Manage the library", with how many works need the person.
- Problems gains "In the library" on a paper-less work, which today has no way to act.
- The column gains each section's one-line description, and `sections.ts` ends at four.
- `model.md`'s frame paragraph, the orientation and every string naming an old section follow.

## Verification, each phase

- `scripts/verify full`;
- a hand check on scratch relloc and synthetic copies with HOME isolated, with screenshots;
- arras re-vendored, and fixtures refreshed when the manifest changes.

## Implementation

### 0.26.1 · built 2026-11-04

- **Arras.**
  - `routes/review/proposed/+page.svelte` and `lib/review/proposed.ts`: `cardsOf` lists a pull, the copies, suggestions with text and citations newest first, then each work with results proposed; `proposedCount` is what Review counts. A copy's card says where its changes stand (`copySays`), a suggestion's shows its change with Take it and Leave it, a citation's shows its claim and work, and a work's opens the queue.
  - A copy, a pull and the queue open in the page at `?copy=`, `?pull` and `?work=`, under a link back. `ContributionReview`, `IncomingPull` and `ProposalQueue` are unchanged.
  - Without a session the page says so and the verbs are disabled, as Library's citation list did.
  - Library lost its queue and its citation list. Its count and "needs you" filter now leave out results to check, which Proposed counts, so nothing counts twice.
  - `VerbRow` and `AnnotationBox` say take it, leave it, taken and left.
- **Loom.** Wording only: refusals say "reload Review", and `loom sync`'s help says the viewer's Review.
- **Departures:**
  - **A suggestion with text resolved without being taken reads "left" in its box**, whoever resolved it, since that is what the verb now means.
  - **Labels of the form "Incoming from …" stay on the manifest's contributions.** They name what is coming in, not a section, and a pull's card shows them.
- **Checked by hand on synthetic and relloc copies:**
  - Synthetic lists 3 to decide and 2 to consider, and the column counts 3. Leave it, its Undo and Take it each act and answer; Take it wrote `sy-0002.tex`, kept versions of main.tex and talk.tex, and the edit appeared among the changes. A citation taken wrote a reference note. A copy opens at its address and comes back.
  - **Relloc holds nothing that decision 9 was written for:** its three open suggestions on the copy are prose, so they stand under To consider and the fold has nothing to hold. The fold is covered by tests on the fixture.

### 0.26.2 · built 2026-11-04

- **Loom.**
  - `looks.py`: `blocks_of` says which statements and proofs each diff group touches, from its changed lines against the scan's offsets; lines removed just outside a block touch nothing. `changes[]` publishes `blocks` and `documents`.
  - `changes-keep` and `changes-undo` take `hunks`, a list, and `loom changes --hunk` may repeat. The ledger's `keep` and `undo` lines carry `hunks`.
  - `review-accept` keeps every group touching only blocks just accepted (`keep_accepted`), with one version kept.
- **Arras.**
  - `lib/review/yourText.ts` joins the changes with `unresolved`; `routes/review/+page.svelte` is rewritten on it, and `/review/changes` is gone. `DiffView` draws named groups (`only`) and reads its diff again when `version` changes; `EditView` folds an edit where a page lists many.
  - Rows are grouped as Changed, each source taken in from, Outside any block, Other files, and the folds Never accepted and Accepted.
  - The column counts each block once, each file's text outside any block, and each file in no document (`yourTextCount`).
- **Departures:**
  - **`loom accept` leaves the look alone.** The plan said accepting keeps; only the viewer's Accept does, since it shows the edit it keeps and a terminal shows none (K4).
  - **A block taken in stays in its source's group** rather than joining Changed, its edit folded. Decision 4 leaves everything taken in listed as an edit, so otherwise a large copy's blocks would all leave their group and all draw a diff.
  - **Changes' "nothing to compare against yet" is gone.** Before a first look the page simply lists blocks.
- **Checked by hand on synthetic and relloc copies:**
  - With one block edited, one sentence of prose added and the fixture's note, synthetic reads 7 waiting. Orbits is listed once with both reasons and its own diff, the prose stands under Outside any block, and the note under Other files. Keep cleared the edit tag, left Accept in place and kept versions of main.tex and talk.tex.
  - Relloc opens on examples.tex with 100 taken in, one group, no diff drawn until asked for.

### 0.26.3 · built 2026-11-04

- **Arras.**
  - `routes/review/record/` has a layout with the heading and tabs, and pages for Versions, Sessions and Everything recorded. The acts page chooses a document in place of the old checkbox.
  - The Library page moved to `/library` unchanged, read under Docs. The shelf's link says "Manage the library" and how many works need the person.
  - Problems links a work with no paper to its row in the library.
  - `sections.ts` ends at Your text, Proposed to you, Problems and Record, each with its line. Record counts a version no document leads to, and its title says how many sessions are open.
- **Docs.** `model.md`'s frame paragraph and the library's paragraph, and every string naming an old section. The orientation's sentence changed, so the shipped modes hash did too.
- **Checked by hand on a synthetic copy:** the column reads as the mockup did, each count beside its name. The three tabs draw, the acts narrow to one document at an address of their own, and "In the library" lands on that work's row with its upload, fetch and ignore.

This comes from versions kept since 0.25.5, not from this plan.
