# 0.17.4 — compare

The last of plan 0.17's four parts: the rail's **compare**, drawn disabled since 0.16, made live. The design is settled in [compare-study.md](../reports/compare-study.md) and summarised as decisions 20–26 in [0.17-ai-drafting-and-compare.md](0.17-ai-drafting-and-compare.md), whose "final compare design" section holds the screenshots this plan builds to (`images/0.17/`, with the demo page as `demo.html`).

**Starts after [0.17.3](0.17.3-ikmartin.md)**, so that landmarks come from stamps before compare reads them, and so after 0.17.1. Independent of Luisa's [0.17.2](0.17.2-luisa.md): compare reads bases through `History.bases`, which already counts the `adopt` and `refresh` lines 0.17.2 writes, so an adopted copy is compared correctly whenever 0.17.2 lands. Work is on its own branch from `main`, merged in before each phase. Each phase is implemented, verified with `scripts/verify full`, recorded under Delivery, and summarised before the next begins.

## Settled in planning

- **Compare's texts are rendered by loom on request**, decided with the author on 2026-09-27. Pressing compare asks the serving publisher for the differing pairs of the two focused items; nothing more is rendered at build, so a build, and `loom serve`'s rebuild on every save, cost what they do today. Rendering every pair at build was weighed and refused: a node gives a pair for every two differing texts among its landmarks, today and each agent copy, which grows with the square of the landmarks (about 3,500 pairs for 340 nodes, 10 landmarks and 3 copies, about 31,000 at worst), and every pair that includes today's text would render again on each save of its node, some ten times a save's cost now, for comparisons nobody opens.
- **What the build adds is cheap.** Every node element in every fragment — draft, agent copy, landmark — gains `data-pair`, its key with any derived suffix stripped, and `data-hash`, its text's `mathematical_hash` (decision 28), beside the attributes it has (`render/fragments.py:358-422`); landmarks still carry no `data-key` or `data-id`, so no live wiring reaches them. A derived node's manifest `base` gains `math`, the base version's `mathematical_hash`, so that a viewer can tell a node changed on both sides without asking loom.
- **The endpoint is `compare`**, a POST that, like `locate` (`render/api.py:197`), writes nothing a record holds, listed in `CAPABILITIES` (`:19`) so that `GET /_api` says whether it is served. Its body is `{left, right}`, each an item as arras addresses it: a document's path, or a landmark as `DOC@STEP`. Loom scans once, pairs the two items' nodes by plain key, and renders each pair whose mathematical hashes differ with the word diff and change marks of Review's comparisons (`render/review_compare.py:22-57`; `render/convert.py:367`, `:507`), each side with its own macro set. The answer lists, per differing pair, its plain key, the two hashes, its direction (which side is the base, changed on both sides since the base, or none — from `History.bases`, `History.copy_of` and the landmarks' step order) and its two fragment paths. A pair differing only in a display name is no difference (decision 28), and is not in the answer. Presence and order are not in the answer: arras computes them.
- **Each rendering is cached by its inputs**, at `build/compare/<hash>.html`, the hash taken over loom's version and code (`_code_hash`, `render/build.py:204`), the two texts and their macro sets. A pair renders once however many comparisons ask for it, a request whose pairs are all cached renders nothing, and a file there is never stale, since its name is its inputs. The directory is outside the build's prune (`render/build.py:476`) and goes with `build/`.
- **With nothing serving** — a static host (DR-161), or before the answer arrives — arras compares from what the fragments and the manifest carry: `data-hash` says which paired nodes differ, `copy_of`, `base` and the landmarks' steps give the direction, and a node that differs is marked on every line, with no words washed. The counter, stepping, presence, order, moves, hover and the jump are the same either way. When the answer arrives each node's marks narrow in place to the lines and words that changed.
- **Compare composes in arras.** For the two focused items it pairs nodes by `data-pair`, and, for each pair the answer names, swaps the node's body in each pane for the pair's highlighted rendering, typeset with its own macros (`isolatedMacros`, `arras/src/lib/fragments/Fragment.svelte:187`). Presence and order it computes itself, from the order of `data-pair` in the two documents: the nodes outside a longest common subsequence are the ones marked, "moved" when one side is a copy of the other (`copy_of`), "order differs" otherwise. The gutter is drawn per pane over the pane's scroller (`PaneContext`, `workspace/state.svelte.ts:43`).
- **A wedge stands where the base places a node**: after the partner of the nearest preceding node that pairs, and at the document's head when none precedes it.
- **Two versions of one key are not compared here.** Nothing opens `key@N` today (`workspace/item.ts:10-39`), and a node item that shows a recorded version belongs to the history view, [WQ-25](../work-queue/WQ-25-history-view.md), decided with the author on 2026-09-27. Compare reaches every version a landmark kept, through the landmark.
- **The filter's `off`** is a third value of `sessionView.view` (`sessions/sessions.svelte.ts:23`), kept by `load()` (`:34`) and answered by `visible()` (`:99`), with a third button in `SessionFilter.svelte` (`:23-25`). The value compare displaced is saved in the same record, so a reload while comparing still restores it on leaving; compare itself is in the workspace's URL beside `?beside` (`workspace/store.svelte.ts:169`).
- **Compare is disabled below the narrow width** (`Workspace.svelte:21`), where only one pane is drawn, and while either focused item is a paper, a session or a node's context; its title says which.
- **Keys `[` and `]`** are bound by the rail while comparing, ignoring modifiers and typing, since `e`, `h` and `s` live in the focused fragment (`Fragment.svelte:294`) and a step concerns both panes.
- **Double-click** is handled on each fragment's root while comparing: the node is `closest('[data-pair]')`, a double-click on an annotation mark keeps its own meaning (`fragments/mount.ts:229`), and the flash is `travel.ts`'s (`:18`).
- **Names:** where a node has a display name (DR-301-luisa), compare's tags and the counter's tooltip use it, as Graph and Review do.

## Behaviour that must survive

- The annotation filter's `this session` and `all` behave as today.
- A landmark carries no live wiring.
- Review's own comparisons (a stale key, an incoming change) are unchanged.
- A build renders what it renders today, and a save under `loom serve` re-renders what it does today.

## Risks

- **The first press waits** for the pairs not yet cached: a pair is two fragments, about 120 ms of CPU (a cold build of `demos/kpsv` renders some 600 fragments in 37 s of CPU), so a comparison of 5 to 40 differing pairs takes about 0.1 to 0.5 s on eight cores, and the request's scan costs what a cached build of the same quilt does (0.5 s for kpsv). The whole-node marks are drawn meanwhile, so the panes never wait blank. If a long document against an old landmark is too slow, the answer renders the pairs in view first.
- **`build/compare/` grows** with every pair ever asked for. It holds only what someone compared, and goes with `build/`; if it becomes large, it is pruned by age.

## Delivery

### Phase 1 · What loom gives compare (done)

- **The pair hash** is `pair_hash` (`scan/hashing.py`): `mathematical_hash` with every derived label made plain, so an agent's copy of a node it has not changed hashes as its counterpart does, which `mathematical_hash` alone would not (every `\label` and `\ref` in a copy carries `-ai`). The label patterns the copy rewrites moved to `scan/labels.py` with one `rename_labels`, which `derive_labels` and `pair_hash` share.
- **`data-pair` and `data-hash`** on every environment and proof whose key names an id (`FragmentRenderer._pair_attrs`), and on a landmark's environments through the converter's `pairs` (`load_canon` reads each reached key's version from the landmark's step; a proof takes the statement before it). `specs/dialect.md` §2.4 and §2.5.
- **`base.math`** on a derived node (`render/manifest.py`), `specs/manifest.md`.
- **`compare`** (`render/compare.py`, `render/api.py`): the two sides resolved (a live document, or a landmark by any of its names), pairs by plain key, a pair only where the hashes differ, the base per pair (the side equal to a derived node's base, `both` when neither is, else the earlier landmark), each side rendered through Review's `_render` with a word diff that counts a derived label as its plain one, cached at `build/compare/<hash>.html`. `specs/write-api.md`.
- **Changed from the plan:** a display name changed alone is no pair, since `data-hash` drops it as decision 28 does; the plan had it as a pair with no highlighted words.

### Phase 3 · The comparison (done)

- **The plan** is pure (`workspace/compare.ts`): pairs, the base per pair (the publisher's answer; else a derived node's `base.math`; else the comparison's base side: a copy's source, the earlier landmark, a person's document against an agent's), marks, wedges, the longest common subsequence for order, and the differences in the left pane's order. **The state** (`compare.svelte.ts`) holds what each pane found, the answer, the step, hover and jumps; the rail asks the publisher once per pair of items, and only where `can('compare')`.
- **The layer** (`CompareLayer.svelte`, drawn in each pane's body while comparing) reads the pane's `[data-pair][data-hash]` in document order, with the number of the last numbered heading before each; puts classes and tags on the nodes; swaps a node's body for the publisher's rendering, typeset with its side's macros, except for a node holding other nodes; and draws the gutter over the body's scroll height, one glyph per line measured from the text's and the formulas' boxes, and wedges sorted and stacked. Presence and order are rules drawn beside the node (a pseudo-element), since every format already uses a node's border. Hover, double-click and stepping are handled on the pane's body.
- **Changed from the plan:** node items compare with node items (the endpoint takes a node's key, and a landmark's path, which is what arras holds); the publisher does not rebuild after `compare` (`READS`); the colours are `--diff-del` and `--diff-add`, git's, in `theme.css`.
- **Found on the way:** `biber`'s unpacking cache under the temp directory was corrupted during a parallel TeX run and failed every biblatex compile silently; clearing it fixed the TeX tier. Nothing in the repository changed for it.
- **Tests:** `workspace/compare.spec.ts` (ten: the longest common subsequence, both kinds of comparison, directions, wedges, moves, the order of differences, the answer's direction and narrowing); `tests/e2e/compare.e2e.ts` (seven, static: disabled and why, `off` and back, a copy against its source, stepping and double-click to one height, presence and order, a landmark against today, a changed tab ending it); `e2e-write/write.e2e.ts` (under `loom serve`: the swapped renderings with their words washed by side, cached, and not rendered twice).
