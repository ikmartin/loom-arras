# 0.6 — The running requests: arras, as used

## Context

`docs/long-prompts/running-requests.md` is the author's list of what grated while using arras on real papers. Nine requests, all in arras; nothing here changes what loom publishes, so the interface version and the conformance fixture are untouched. The agent-review design that was in progress when this round started is paused and pinned as [WQ-23](../work-queue/WQ-23-agent-review.md).

## The requests, and what each becomes

1. **Comments as expandable highlights.** A display preference, `comments: margin | inline`, default `margin` (the request asks for the option). In both modes a mark is coloured by its comment's kind. Inline, a mark expands the comment in the text beneath its paragraph, and a comment with no mark (no quote, or a detached anchor) is reached from a count beside its node's label. Selecting outside or pressing Escape closes it.
2. **The layered graph.** Fix the coordinate frames: ELK reports an edge relative to the lowest common ancestor of its endpoints, and the page read every edge as if it were at the root, which is why grouped edges floated free of their nodes. Draw a section that is both a group and an endpoint once, as the group. Nodes are not draggable in the layered layout; dragging there pans.
3. **A local graph.** The neighbourhood of one node, drawn live with `d3-force` in the manner of Quartz's graph view (hover dims all but a node's neighbours, click opens, depth 1 or 2). On a node page it heads the right rail. In the read view it is a panel the reader opens, and its centre follows the node at the reading line. Either expands to a larger overlay. Written for arras rather than ported: Quartz draws with PixiJS, and arras ships inside loom's wheel.
4. **Clickable references.** A citation with no digest target links to its reference page instead of rendering as inert text. The references page and each reference page link the work out — `doi:` to doi.org, `arXiv:` to arxiv.org, `mr:` and `zbl:` to their databases, a bibliography `url` as itself — and to a fetched PDF when the manifest says one exists. BibTeX's protective braces are removed from displayed titles.
5. **The accepted, stale, incomplete and errors pages.** The home cards open the review table filtered to that state (`/review?show=…`) and the problems page filtered to errors (`/problems?severity=error`); filters live in the URL and in the left panel on both pages. The blockers page is folded into the review table: `show=incomplete` gains a column naming what each gap blocks, and `/blockers` redirects there. The icon strip's panel no longer lists the views it sits beside: a page with nothing to register shows the document and its contents.
6. **Retire the tabs shell.** Two shells remain, rail and strip; a stored or linked `b` falls back to the default.
7. **Select outside to close.** One action shared by every floating box: the settings panel, the help panels, expanded comments, previews, the expanded local graph.
8. **Hover previews.** The site generator's behaviour, ported: hovering a link to a node or a reference for 300ms shows a card with its label, state and statement, typeset; the card stays while the pointer is in it, and Escape or scrolling dismisses it. Covers references in the text, rail lists (depends on, used by, see also) and every table.
9. **Speed.** Measure first on `demos/relloc` (upgraded, on a copy). Expected: the manifest held as `$state.raw` rather than a deep proxy; MathJax loaded while idle instead of on first use; polling paused while the tab is hidden. Anything else only with a measurement behind it.
