# 0.30 — Annotations: one record, types, and changes reviewed in the document

## Context

On 2026-11-09, 25 annotations in the quant quilt detached at once because a proof with no statement took its key from a character offset. The key was fixed that day; the anchoring was not. An annotation re-finds its exact quote (`resolve_selector`, `records/selectors.py`), so an edit inside the quote detaches it, and although `loom annotate` freezes the text it was written against (`_version`, `cli/review.py`), nothing shows that text, so a settled annotation drops off the page once its words change.

The author then set a direction and worked it out through an HTML prototype, published privately as the annotation demo (versions 11 to 28, https://claude.ai/artifact/8tdq957EHHhjqByoKMwe7X). This plan builds what the demo settled. Today a person meets three records and two vocabularies:

- **Findings** in `.loom/annotations/log.jsonl`: an annotation with a kind (objection, suggestion, question, citation, note), a severity on two of them, an optional payload of replacement text, a session, and a discard beside the resolve.
- **Notes** in `.loom/annotations/notes.jsonl` (`notes.py`, `loom note`): quotes that fade after 14 days, notes on a whole work, and since plan 0.28 the marks drawn on a PDF page.
- **Your Changes** (`looks.py`): an agent's edits to an open file, kept or undone per hunk in Review, apart from the suggestions in the log that carry the same kind of change and are taken in Proposed Changes.

On a document, hue is fixed to kind (A4), so a person cannot colour a highlight; only a mark on a PDF page takes a colour, one of five. The modes name kinds that the person cannot rename or extend; the quant quilt needs a `hint` that no kind provides.

There are no users to migrate and no shims (the author's standing rule). The quant quilt holds the only real records: 75 annotations created (52 objections, 21 suggestions, 2 notes), 46 discarded and 3 resolved, so 26 live, and no notes file.

## How this plan is carried out

The author approved this plan on 2026-11-11. This file is the one master plan. Each phase is planned concretely in plan mode when it starts and recorded in `## Implementation` below once `scripts/verify full` passes. The author decides afresh whether later phases may run back to back.

It starts after plan 0.29 is merged, since marks on a document sit on 0.29's HTML documents and both plans use the word alignment in `tex/align.py` (0.29, decision 7), and after the whole-file locks branch (`claude/cool-carson-5585d3`) is merged, since deleting an annotation rewrites its log.

## Decisions

The author made each of these between 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-11, most of them against the demo. The author's approval of this plan also approves the changes to principles and invariants named under each.

1. **One record: the annotation.** A mark on words (underline, highlight or strike), a comment at a place, or a mark drawn on a PDF page, on a document, a cited work or a PDF. Notes and PDF marks join it, written through the annotation log's one path (P4); `notes.jsonl`, `loom note` and fading retire (P1, P3, K1). An annotation may also be on a whole thing (a key, a document, a work), as a finding about a whole key is today.
2. **Quoting is not a record.** Selecting words with no tool on offers Quote, which hands the words and where they came from to an agent, as highlighting part of a reply does in a chat. How a quote reaches an agent's session directly belongs to the replies plan; until then it is copied, with its place.
3. **An annotation carries its own look**: one of nine colours (grey, brown, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, purple, pink), a style (underline, highlight or strike), a thickness of 1 to 5 px in whole pixels, and an opacity. A colour means whatever its reader decides; A4 and A6 are rewritten, since hue is no longer the kind and weight no longer the severity. Strike is a style for every annotation, on documents and PDFs alike; ink, words on the page, box, oval and arrow stay PDF tools.
4. **Types are presets of the quilt, and optional.** `.loom/annotation-types.toml`, written by `loom init` with the defaults listed and committed with the quilt, holds each type's name, look, whether it stays open until resolved or only informs, whether it is listed in Notes, and its instructions for agents. P6 allows it: the preamble cannot say any of this, and `config.toml` is the person's, edited line by line, while instructions run to paragraphs. The person edits it in Settings › Annotations and an agent may edit the file ("Where an agent may write is one table" names it); `loom upgrade` never overwrites it ("Upgrade never destroys an author's edit"). An annotation names its type by a fixed id and keeps its own look, so renaming a type touches no annotation, and deleting one leaves its annotations untyped and looking as they did. No interface depends on a particular type existing.
5. **The default types** are Revision (red highlight, 65%: something is wrong and must change, with its seriousness said in the comment), Suggestion (orange, 60%: nothing is wrong but it could be better), Question (blue, 60%) and Note (grey, 60%, only informs, listed in Notes). Kinds and severity retire: the demo merged Revision (major) and (minor) because nothing treated a minor revision differently from a suggestion, and a citation is a suggestion that names the work.
6. **Open, resolved and To do.** Only an annotation whose type stays open until resolved is open; an untyped annotation never is. Only those have Resolve, and only they appear in To do, beside the changes still to keep or discard. `loom status` counts open annotations by type, and its "reviewed clean" fact goes, since a plain highlight on a key would otherwise make it look reviewed (P15).
7. **Delete deletes.** An annotation is removed from the log rather than marked discarded, so the log stops being append-only for annotations: P2 is edited, and the invariant "Review records are appended, never rewritten" gains a dated line. The `discarded` event, `--discard` and the `discard` endpoint go for annotations. An agent deletes only its own annotations, as it withdraws its own findings today; deleting by author or by version is the person's (K5), and replaces `loom ai discard SESSION` and what `loom session delete --purge` does to annotations. The viewer's undo keeps what it deleted until the page closes.
8. **Annotations leave sessions.** Each carries its author, its time and its version, and `annotate` stops naming or writing a session. The transcript stays with replies, in the replies plan.
9. **An annotation shows the version it was written on.** Loom already freezes that text, stored by hash, so a batch of annotations on unchanged words keeps one copy and only an edit between two annotations makes a second; a PDF mark keeps the file's hash (A3). What is new is showing it: the current text draws the annotations whose words survive, an annotation whose words changed says it was written on earlier words and opens the version it was written on, and the version draws all its annotations, so anything loom keeps, a resolved annotation among it, stays reachable from where its words were (V3).
10. **Suggested edits are a record of their own.** A suggested edit replaces old words with new, carries an optional comment, and may name the annotation it answers; an annotation no longer carries a payload. Since the payload leaves in 0.30.0, the record and the command an agent files it with arrive there too.
11. **Changes are reviewed in the document.** Markup shows every pending change in place, removed words struck and new words underlined in teal, a colour annotations never use; Final shows the text with every change made. Each change has Keep and Discard (K7: one pair of names, where today there are Keep and Undo, Take it and Leave it); a Changes menu holds the two views, Keep all and Discard all. An agent's in-place edits since the person's look and suggested edits are reviewed the same way, and "current" means what the person has kept. Review › Your Changes lists the same changes across the quilt with the same card, Review gains a quilt-wide To do, and Proposed Changes keeps agent documents and library proposals but no longer suggestions (V2).
12. **What Keep does to annotations.** Keep carries every annotation the change touches onto the new words, marked as written on earlier words. An annotation whose words were all removed stays as a mark at that spot. Keeping a change that names an annotation resolves it, with the change as the reason; Discard leaves annotations as they are, and loom resolves nothing otherwise.
13. **Agents read the types, not kinds.** Items 4 to 6 of `rules.md` become a section that `loom ai orient` and the MCP server's instructions generate from the types file, plus the rule for suggested edits. A mode names a type through a placeholder that loom fills with its current name, so a rename reaches every mode and loom knows which modes use a type, which the warning on deleting one needs. A type's instructions say only when to use it; the rules for every annotation stay in `rules.md`, with one new line: use the type whose instructions fit, and leave an annotation untyped when none does.
14. **Notes is a list loom builds** (P3): every annotation whose type is listed in Notes, from every document, grouped by document, each linked back to where it was left. Nobody edits it.
15. **Works with only a PDF**, the scope the author set on 2026-11-09: rectangles and the file's hash stay the anchor of record; the words and gaps around a mark are re-found anywhere in the document; and where source exists, printed words are aligned to it. An agent reading a mark gets a crop of the page, made by `pdftocairo` into the build directory and sent as an image over MCP. Loom calls no model, so TeX an agent reads off a crop is its claim (P8).

Not in this plan: replies and hooks into agent clients (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini's CLI), a plan of their own; an annotated LaTeX or PDF export (WQ-28); bodies or marks beside the manifest (WQ-36, WQ-65).

## Phases

### 0.30.0 · One record, and types

Loom's side, with the viewer kept working.

- **The record** (`records/annotations.py`, `records/log.py`, `records/store.py`): an annotation loses `kind`, `severity`, `payload`, `placement` and `session`, and gains `type`, its look and the version it names; a mark keeps `page`, `rects`, `strokes`, `artifact` and `origin`. `KINDS`, `ASKING`, `SEVERITIES`, `GRADED`, `PLACEMENTS` and `full_kind` go, and so do the `discarded` event and the withdrawal of a whole session by `source`. Deleting rewrites the log under the lock. `_review_facts` counts open annotations by type, and `_facts` (`cli/review.py`) drops "reviewed clean".
- **Notes join it.** `notes.py`, `cli/note_cmd.py` and `notes.jsonl` go; a mark and its change, erase and restore become annotation events, and `take_in` imports into the annotation log.
- **Types**: a new module reads `.loom/annotation-types.toml`, falling back to the defaults with a diagnostic when the file is missing or malformed. `write_minimal_quilt` (`cli/quilt.py`) writes it, and `loom upgrade` writes it where it is absent.
- **Suggested edits**: the record (from `records/suggestions.py`, whose placing and patching it keeps) and the command an agent files one with. Proposed Changes keeps its Take it on them until 0.30.3.
- **Commands**: `loom annotate` takes `--type` and the look, and `--delete`, with deletion by author or version; `--kind`, `--severity`, `--payload`, `--placement`, `--discard` and `--session` go. `loom ai discard`, the annotation part of `loom session delete --purge`, and the kind and severity filters on `loom status` and `loom ai annotations` give way to type, author and version.
- **API and manifest** (`render/api.py` `_review` and `_note`, `render/build.py`): `discard`, `note` and its family go; `annotate` takes the type and look, and new endpoints delete and restyle. The manifest carries the types and each annotation's look, and drops `notes`.
- **Agents**: the rules, the orientation, `formatting.md` and the modes change here, because the commands they teach change. `ai/orient.py` generates the types section for `loom ai orient` and the MCP server's instructions; modes take placeholders; `--discard` becomes `--delete` in rule 7. `since.py` reports annotations by type and drops its notes section.
- **The viewer, at a minimum**: marks are drawn from their own look (`fragments/mount.ts`, `theme.css`), `lib/review/kinds.ts` gives way to the manifest's types, the composer (`pdf/NoteAt.svelte`) writes a type, and the PDF desk writes through `annotate`.

*Accept:* `loom init` writes the types file with four types; thirty annotations on an unchanged key keep one frozen text, whether sent as one `--batch` or thirty MCP calls; a deleted annotation leaves no line in the log, and the log and `loom history verify` still pass; an agent deleting another's annotation is refused; renaming a type in the file changes the name every mode prints; a malformed types file loads the defaults and says so; `loom status` never says "reviewed clean"; `scripts/verify full` passes.

### 0.30.1 · Annotating in the viewer

Everything the demo settled for documents: the toolbar and format menu, the one popup, margin icons with the line chooser, the stepper, the Comments list with To do and the filter, the Notes list, Quote on a bare selection, Settings › Annotations as a section of the Quilt group (`shell/settings/catalog.ts`) that writes the types file, and the version view behind "Written on earlier words". What this replaces is deleted, not kept beside it: the box (`components/AnnotationBox.svelte`), its verbs (`review/VerbRow.svelte`), the selection chip (`fragments/AnnotateChip.svelte`), the notes fold (`workspace/items/NotesHere.svelte`), and the kind hues in `pdf/Reading.svelte`, `sessions/sessions.svelte.ts` and `ContextItem.svelte`. No component reads a type's id to decide what to show.

*Accept:* on a scratch copy of `hull_ch1` in the running viewer, every interaction the demo shows works, in light and dark mode; deleting a type leaves its annotations looking as they did; an annotation whose quoted words were edited outside loom shows its version and stays reachable from its place; Notes links back to a mark on a PDF and on a document.

### 0.30.2 · PDFs

PDF marks become annotations in the viewer as well: the same toolbar, popup and lists on a PDF page, with the PDF's own tools beside them. `pdf/marks.ts` takes the nine colours, and `portable.ts` maps them by hue on import and export, as it already does strike-outs. A work with only a PDF anchors by rectangles and hash, re-finds a mark from the words and gaps around it anywhere in the document, and aligns printed words to source where there is source. A crop of the page around each mark is made with `pdftocairo` into the build directory and reaches an agent wherever it reads the mark, as an image over MCP.

*Accept:* a mark survives a re-saved PDF whose words moved by up to a line; taking in a PDF marked in Preview yields annotations in the nearest of the nine colours, strike-outs as strike; an agent over MCP is given a crop for a mark; the PDF-only fixture shows every mark it holds.

### 0.30.3 · Suggested edits and changes in the document

Markup and Final draw every pending change, built on 0.29's changed-word marks through alignment, with Keep, Discard, the Changes menu, Keep all and Discard all. An agent's in-place edits since the person's look (`looks.py`, `changes-keep` and `changes-undo`, and `loom changes` on the command line) are reviewed per change in the same place, beside suggested edits, and Keep carries annotations onto the new words (decision 12). Review › Your Changes (`routes/review/+page.svelte`, `review/yourChanges.ts`) becomes the quilt-wide list of the same cards with a quilt-wide To do, and suggestions leave Proposed Changes (`routes/review/proposed`, `review/suggestions.ts`). WQ-62 closes: Final is a document read with its suggestions folded in.

*Accept:* on a scratch copy of `hull_ch1` with one in-place edit and one suggested edit, both show in Markup and Final and in Review, Keep and Discard behave the same from either place, an annotation on edited words moves to the new words marked as written on earlier ones, and Keep resolves the annotation a change names; `scripts/verify full` passes.

## Risks and what would falsify this

1. **A deleted annotation returns through a merge.** The log travels with git, and a coauthor's copy still holds the deleted lines. If it happens in use, a deletion leaves a hidden tombstone after all, and decision 7 goes back to the author.
2. **Carrying annotations needs a known change.** Keep carries them because loom knows the change; an author's own edit in their editor is not one, so those annotations fall back to their version, as they detach today. Falsified if most edits happen outside loom and annotations end up living on their versions.
3. **An agent's edit to the types file changes the quilt's vocabulary.** Renaming is safe through the ids, but an agent removing a type a mode uses leaves that mode filing untyped annotations. The file is an ordinary file, so the edit shows in Your Changes; if agents do this unasked, the file becomes the person's.
4. **Nine colours do not survive export.** Other readers may redraw colours or drop opacity. Checked in 0.30.2 against Preview and Skim.
