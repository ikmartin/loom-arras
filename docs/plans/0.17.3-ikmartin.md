# 0.17.3 — canon folds into the history

The third of plan 0.17's four parts: canonizing and stamping become one act, a landmark is a stamp that stores its document's text, `loom history` shows and restores a landmark, and a paper brought in is drafted at once. `loom draft` keeps only the job [0.17.1](0.17.1-sequential-ikmartin.md) gave it, the copy across the border. The decisions (15–18) and their reasons are in [0.17-ai-drafting-and-compare.md](0.17-ai-drafting-and-compare.md) and ai-drafting-study §10.

**Starts after 0.17.1 is merged into `main`**, since both rewrite `loom draft`; runs in parallel with Luisa's [0.17.2](0.17.2-luisa.md), and comes before [0.17.4](0.17.4-ikmartin.md), so that compare is built once against the final source of landmarks. Work is on its own branch from `main`, merged in before each phase. Each phase is implemented, verified with `scripts/verify full`, recorded under Delivery, and summarised before the next begins.

## Settled in planning

- **A landmark is a stamp given a document.** Every stamp already requires a message (`cli/history_cmds.py:320`), so the study's "a named stamp is a landmark" would make every stamp one. Instead `loom stamp DOC -m NAME` freezes the keys DOC reaches, as `--in DOC` does today, and stores DOC's flat text in the step (`write_step`'s `document_text`, `history/steps.py:125`); that stamp is a landmark. `loom stamp -m NAME` with no document freezes what moved quilt-wide and is not a landmark. `--in` goes, since the positional document says the same.
- **A landmark's text is the loom form**: the flattened document with `\usepackage{loom}` and its ids, what `flatten` gives (`reshape/linearize.py:31`), so `restore` can draft from it. The paper without loom, which `canonize` wrote to `canon/`, is `loom history show DOC@STEP --plain` (`to_canon`, `reshape/linearize.py:89`).
- **A landmark is addressed `DOC@STEP`**, the document's stem and the step's number, as arras already labels one (`stem @N`, `arras/src/lib/workspace/registry.ts:44-49`).
- **`canon/`, `[quilt] canon` and `loom canonize` are removed** *(to confirm with the author)*. Once a stamp stores the text, the canon file is derived and nothing needs it; the no-backwards-compatibility rule applies, and the fixtures and demos are regenerated. The synthetic quilt's landmarks are already canonize steps with their text stored, so they become landmarks as they stand.
- **arras is unchanged apart from a word.** Landmark entries in the manifest keep `CanonDoc`'s shape (`arras/src/lib/manifest/types.ts:42`), built from stamp steps instead of canon files, so the landmark item, its `/canon/` route (`workspace/item.ts:83`) and its fragment are as they are; the panel's Canon subsection is headed Landmarks (`shell/IconStrip.svelte:187`).

## Phase 1 · Stamps store the text; `history show` and `restore`

- `loom stamp [DOC] -m NAME` as settled (`cli/history_cmds.py:320`).
- `loom history show DOC@STEP [--plain]` prints a landmark's text; `loom history restore DOC@STEP --to drafting/NEW.tex` writes it as a new document, author-only, refusing an existing path, and records a `restore` line (`read_version`, `materialize`, `version_at`, `history/versions.py:25-68`; `History.resolve_step`, `history/ledger.py:103`); `loom history` itself (`cli/history_cmds.py:744`) gains the two subcommands beside `verify`. `restore` added to `ACTIONS` (`history/ledger.py:20`) and to `_detail` (`cli/history_cmds.py:794`).
- **Tests:** `tests/unit/history/test_commands.py` — a stamp given a document stores its text and one without does not; `show` and `--plain`; `restore` writes, refuses an existing path, and records its line; `loom history verify` over both.

## Phase 2 · Bringing a paper in drafts it at once

- **Tests:** `test_init.py` and `test_paper.py` — `init --from` and `import` leave a drafted document with ids and a landmark of the original; `draft` without `--ai` is refused with the command that replaced it.

- Every landmark the regenerated fixtures carry opens as before, with its own macros and no live wiring.
- `loom history show DOC@STEP --plain` is byte for byte what `canonize` wrote to `canon/` for the same document and step.
- Every existing stamp keeps its versions; `loom revert KEY@N` works on them.

## Risks

- **The book's reach:** fifteen chapters name canon, and a mechanical rename would leave sentences that describe a directory that no longer exists (the ten passes' first and ninth).
- **Removing `canon/`** touches every fixture and demo; they are regenerated rather than migrated.

## Delivery

The four phases landed together (2026-09-27): removing `canonize` was the only way to regenerate the test quilts, whose landmarks it had made, so the code of phases 1–3 is one change; the Delivery below keeps them apart.

### Phases 1–3 · Landmarks, the import, and `canon/` gone (done)

- **Landmarks:** `loom stamp DOCUMENT -m NAME` keeps DOCUMENT's flat text as `<name>.tex` in its step directory, with `landmark`, `to` and `reaches` on its line, refuses a name already used, and gathers the bibliography as `canonize` did; `loom stamp -m MESSAGE` alone freezes what moved quilt-wide and keeps none. `--in` is gone. `History.landmarks`, `landmark(ref)` (a name, a step, or `DOC@STEP`) and `landmark_path`.
- **`loom history show LANDMARK [--plain]` and `loom history restore LANDMARK --to FILE`**, the latter author-only, drafting through `plan_draft` (which now takes a text) and recording a `restore` line; `loom history verify` checks a restore's landmark.
- **The import** keeps the paper as received as step 0001's landmark and drafts the working document at once, with `--fix-anchoring` on `import` and `init --from`, the node counts printed, and the paper's style files staged beside the draft so that their theorem environments get ids (found by the documentation pass: `relloc` drafted 11 ids before the fix and 52 after, as an in-place import does).
- **Removed:** `loom canonize` and its aliases, `[quilt] canon`, `canon/` in `init` and the scan, `loom:canon-edited`, `draft`'s canon job, `draft` lines and `parent`; the permission file's `canon`. Landmarks render from their step files, keeping `CanonDoc`'s shape, so arras changed only its headings (Landmarks, in the panel and on the home page).
- **Found on the way:** `plan_insertions` gave a second id to a definition demoted because another live document defines its id; it now skips `conflict_of` nodes, so `restore` beside a live copy adds no ids and says the ids are shared.
- **Tests:** `history/test_commands.py` and `test_paper.py` rewritten to the new commands (stamp as landmark, `show`, `restore`, the import's landmark and draft, anchoring on import); `render/test_canon.py`, `refs/test_scan.py`, `history/test_ledger.py`, `tex/test_reshape_real.py`, the paper tier and the invariance test moved to landmarks; a regression test for the double id; the problems-page e2e test adds a record diagnostic of its own, since the fixture's only one was `canon-edited`.
