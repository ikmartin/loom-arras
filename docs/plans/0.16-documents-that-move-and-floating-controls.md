# 0.16 — documents that move, and controls that float

Two things, settled with the author on 2026-09-26: records that name a document follow it when it moves, and an item's controls leave the global rail for a toolbar on the page. Both prepare for 0.17 (AI drafting, `docs/reports/ai-drafting-study.md`), which moves text between documents and puts **compare** on the rail.

## Context

**The record defect.** Acceptance rows record the document they were compiled against (`master`, DR-226-VelascoL), and annotations may record the document they are read in (`in`, DR-291-ikmartin), both by path. When a document moves — `linearize`, `atomize`, a rename in an editor, a collaborator's rename on Overleaf — freshness looks up a preamble that no longer exists (`Records.preamble_hash`, `loom/src/loom/records/store.py:125`), finds none, and reports every key accepted in that document stale with `preamble-changed`, which is false. Confirmed on 2026-09-26: accept `dm-0001` in a copy of the demo quilt, `loom linearize drafting/main.tex --to drafting/main-flat.tex --keep-shared`, and `dm-0001` is stale. The same lookup breaks `loom accept --stale` (it re-accepts against the vanished document with an empty preamble, so the key never becomes fresh), Overleaf sync (it refuses until `.loom/source-sync.json` is edited by hand), and annotations `in` or on a moved document (they detach). 0.17 moves text between documents constantly, so this is fixed first.

**The controls.** The global rail holds the annotation filter and the focused item's cluster: its views, then its controls (book 15.2.5). 0.17 puts **compare**, a control about both documents on screen, on the rail, and there is no room. The design study (`docs/reports/document-controls-study.md`) settled where each thing goes: the rail keeps only what concerns both panes; an item's controls become a corner toolbar on the focused pane; its views stand at the end of its pane's tab strip; tabs narrow, then scroll.

## The decisions

From `docs/reports/ai-drafting-study.md` part three and `docs/reports/document-controls-study.md`; the studies carry the reasoning and the rejected alternatives.

**Documents that move**

1. **Records are resolved when read, never rewritten.** One lookup — where is this document now — follows the history's chain of moves (a conversion's `from` and `to`, and each recorded rename) to the document's current path. The ledger stays append-only; `loom live` undoes a move for free.
2. **A record whose document is gone, with nothing saying where, has its own cause:** "the document it was accepted in is gone", naming the path and the fix, and one `loom lint` diagnostic for all such rows.
3. **`loom mv OLD NEW`, author-only.** `OLD` exists: move the file and record the move. `OLD` gone and `NEW` present: record a rename already made. Moving the default document moves `[quilt] main`, as conversions do.
4. **An Overleaf pull records exact renames:** a drafting document deleted and an identical one added in one pull is a move.
5. **What follows:** acceptance `master`; annotation `in`; annotations whose target is a whole document; the sync record's main and selected documents; `loom accept --stale` is fixed. The Review queue's saved decisions and annotations carried in packets are left as they are.

**Controls that float**

6. **The rail holds only what concerns both panes:** the annotation filter, and **compare**, drawn from 0.16 as a placeholder that arrives in 0.17.
7. **An item's controls are a corner toolbar on the focused pane only**: the annotating tools where a publisher is serving, anything used continually while reading, **view ▾** (the existing Popover, every other control in full words with its key), and the pin. The divider before *view* is drawn only when something stands to its left.
8. **It floats:** it wakes while the pointer is in a band across the top of the pane — 96px tall, inset 96px from the left and 4px from the right, always containing the bar — and fades over 0.4 s when the pointer leaves; it stays while the pointer is on it, while its menu is open, and while pinned; keyboard focus in the pane shows it.
9. **The pin**, pinned by default, is one preference shared by every toolbar and saved with the viewer's other preferences (`arras.prefs`). Off: a tilted outline in the faint ink. On: upright, filled and heavier in the link's colour on its wash. Its tooltip names what a click does.
10. **Views stand at the right end of the pane's tab strip**, plain words, the current one underlined in the accent, the unavailable one greyed; on both panes; never fading.
11. **Tabs narrow, then scroll:** 148px at most; narrowed evenly when the strip is crowded, down to 1.5 times their height; past that the row scrolls, the views keep their place and the active tab is scrolled into view. Below 90px a tab's close shows only on hover.
12. **Show settled on a paper**, since the PDF's marks already honour it.

## Settled in planning

- **One resolver, in the history.** `History` (`loom/src/loom/history/ledger.py`) gains the lookup beside `superseded_paths()` (:117): a path that is a live document is itself; otherwise the latest entry that moved it names its successor, followed until a live document or nothing. Moves are a conversion's `from`/`to` (atomize :305, linearize :585) and the new `move` entries; a `live` line makes a path its own again. Every reader of a document path calls it; nothing else knows about moves.
- **The new cause is `document-gone`**, carried like the others by `Cause` (`records/store.py:28`), and the diagnostic `loom:document-gone` is a record-subject diagnostic with a `Fix` naming `loom mv` (`scan/model.py`, registered in `scan/diagnostics.py` `LOOM`). Book 7.6.2's list of causes grows to seven. Arras draws an unknown cause kind as its words already (`routes/review/+page.svelte:282`), so the viewer needs no change for it.
- **`loom mv` lives in `cli/history_cmds.py`** beside `live` (:468), appending a `move` line (`ACTIONS`, `history/ledger.py:19`) with `from`, `to` and whether loom moved the file; `_detail` prints it in `loom history`. Not in `AGENT_COMMANDS` (`ai/layout.py:31`), so author-only by construction. It moves only drafting documents; a node file or anything else is refused with the reason.
- **Exact renames at a pull** are found where the incoming diff is listed (`sync.py:166`, today `--no-renames`): a deleted and an added drafting document with the same blob. The move is recorded when the pull is incorporated, never on fetch.
- **The rail's compare is drawn disabled** until 0.17, its title saying what it will do and when, since a control that does nothing when pressed would claim what is not so (P3).
- **Controls move by kind, not by rewrite.** The registry (`workspace/registry.ts`) keeps one entry per kind; `controls` splits into what stands on the bar and what goes under *view*, and `views` moves from the rail to the tab strip. `GlobalRail.svelte` keeps the filter and gains compare. The toolbar is one component the focused `Pane.svelte` draws on its own layer; `pdf/ToolPair.svelte` and `pdf/PdfTools.svelte` are reused on the bar and `components/Popover.svelte` for *view*.
- **The testids stay** (`toggle-annotations`, `toggle-settled`, `tool-select`, `tool-box`, `open-context`, `source-toggle`, the zoom and page ids, `tab-paper`, `tab-digest`, `tab-info`, `tab-chat`, `tab-did`), so the suites follow the controls to their new place; what changes is where the tests look, and the tests that assert the old layout.
- **No compatibility code** (the no-backwards-compatibility rule): the rail's cluster, and the fixed 148px, go.

## Behaviour that must survive

- Every existing acceptance stays as fresh as it is now when nothing has moved.
- A superseded document made live again with `loom live` is its own path again.
- Overleaf sees the same published file names after a local move.
- Every control reachable today is reachable after, by the same testid and the same key.
- DR-254-ikmartin: a paper's page tools stay drawn and greyed off its Paper view.
- Only the focused pane shows a toolbar; the unfocused pane shows its views in its strip.

## Risks

- **A long chain of moves** is walked on every read of a record: the resolver memoises per scan.
- **A move to a path that later holds a different document**: the resolver stops at the first live document, so recording a move into an occupied path is refused by `mv`.
- **The floating toolbar over a narrow pane** may cover the first words of a line; the pin off and the band are what keep it out of the way, and the screenshots at half width are the check.
