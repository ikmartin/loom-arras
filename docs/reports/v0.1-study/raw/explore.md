The question worked on: Lemma 4.1 (`sh-000E`, Integrality), "the polytope $\Pi$ of Theorem 3.4 has integral vertices", marked `\incomplete`, on which Theorem 3.4's proof rests. It was worked honestly: Attempt 1 (from saturation) fails, with a counterexample ($\mathbb Z(1,2)\subset\mathbb Z^2$ is saturated and its box polytope has vertices $\pm(1/2,1)$); an exact computation finds no non-integral vertex on any of 787 arrow multisets with at most 3 vertices and 4 arrows; Bellamy19 p.2 has the right argument (total unimodularity of an incidence matrix); Attempt 2 proves the lemma by that argument and Hoffman and Kruskal, which is not in the library. On the way, the showcase's Caveat 3.1 (`sh-000D`) and the loose Lemma `sh-0402` turned out false as stated; that is the demo's mathematics, not loom's, but whoever maintains the showcase should know.

## Findings

**EXP-1. An `\incomplete` step is invisible where the researcher reads.** Weight: hurts. Recommendation: fix. `\incomplete{…}` expands to nothing (`loom/src/loom/assets/loom.sty:11`, `\providecommand{\incomplete}[1]{}`, and the same line in the marked preamble block), so the TeX4ht document shows Lemma 4.1 as an ordinary lemma (shot 01b), the node page shows only the statement (shot 06), and an agent's failed attempt whose reason is an `\incomplete` reads as a finished proof (shot 14). The reason appears in the viewer only folded under Never accepted, as raw TeX (shot 08); a link's hover preview shows a bare "incomplete" badge (`arras/src/lib/badges.ts:54`, used only by `NodePreview.svelte`). In the copy review the change that removes the marker renders identically on both sides (shot 35). The manifest already carries `node.incomplete` (the reasons), so the fix is presentation: a margin mark and the reason on incomplete blocks in `DocumentItem`/`FragmentNotes`, and an HTML branch in `loom.sty` that prints the reason as a marked span. Hours to a day.

**EXP-2. A recorded dead end was not found, and was re-derived two months later.** Weight: hurts (it is the failure the Explorer's design names). Recommendation: fix (rules now, a query option soon). The September Revision "Integrality of the vertices of $\Pi$ does not follow from saturation…" and Wren Halloway's reply sit on `sh-000C/proof`, the proof that uses the gap, not on `sh-000E`, the question. Nothing an agent is told to run gathers them: `loom source sh-000E --closure` prints no annotations; `loom ai annotations --target sh-000E` lists only what is on the lemma (and without `--json` no message text); `loom downstream sh-000E` misses `sh-000C/proof`, which relies on the lemma without `\ref`-ing it; `loom since` gives 21 annotations in time order. The rules say to search the digests before calling something new (rule 9) but not to read what was already tried. Fix: a rule in `loom/src/loom/assets/ai/rules.md` (rule 9) and `modes/question.md`/`draft.md`: before an attempt, read the research state's entry and the annotations on the question and on the keys that state or use it; and a `loom ai annotations --around KEY` (the key, its closure, its downstream, and keys whose annotations link it) in `cli/` and the MCP tool list. Rules: an hour; option: a day.

**EXP-3. Words typed right after marking are lost.** Weight: hurts (capture is the one gesture P14 promises). Recommendation: fix. With a type that only informs (Plain, Note), the comment is meant to start by typing; but the key capture runs only once `marked` is set, and `marked` is set only after `await record(...)` returns (`arras/src/lib/fragments/FragmentNotes.svelte:213`, capture at `:93-118`). Each annotate write waits for the publication lock and a rebuild (`loom/src/loom/render/serve.py:363-368`): three POSTs to `/_api/annotate` on the showcase took 1.29, 1.36 and 1.52 s. Observed: "zonotope? (prob" lost at 20 ms per key; "leading coefficie" lost at 110 ms per key (shots 22, 24). Keys in that window reach the document's shortcuts (`j`/`k` step, digits change type, space scrolls). Fix: set a provisional mark and buffer keys before the write, then hand them to the popup. Hours. Two smaller faults met here: a drag starting inside "polynomial" (italic theorem text) saved the quote "lynomial" despite the snap-to-words rule (cosmetic); and a comment typed into an existing annotation's popup is dropped silently when the page is left without Save (cosmetic).

**EXP-4. Taking a working proof from an agent copy needs a terminal, and leaving the failed attempt beside it is impossible.** Weight: blocks the copy route for step 8 on the showcase; hurts generally. Recommendation: fix the messages and the all-or-nothing rule; for v0.1, route a single finished proof through a suggested edit. In Review › Proposed Changes, with the statement and the second proof taken and Attempt 1 left, Apply said "sh-000E/proof/2 is a new result, and placing it changes the document; run `loom adopt integrality.tex --document-changes`, with the keys you chose" (`adopt.py:535`). Taking "Prose and ordering" (whose only content is `% !LOOM adopt-node:` markers, shown to the person as its source) then gave "sh-000E/proof is missing: select its proposal or revise the document-level changes" (`adopt.py:309`). After the agent deleted Attempt 1 from the copy (its prose then survives nowhere in loom), Take all and Apply gave "proof is neither adjacent to a statement nor names one with \ref" (`scan/nodes.py:515`): the new proof is placed by its marker in the section's spine `nodes/sh-0400.tex`, after `\input{nodes/sh-000E}`, a different file from its statement. Not checked on a single-file paper: running `loom adopt` as the person was refused in this environment. What worked instead: two suggested edits, keeping each in Review; `--answers` resolved the person's question on Keep. That route needs `--quote '\end{lemma}' --after`, which `rules.md` tells agents not to do (CUT-14b). Files: `adopt.py` (no command in a message; drop a left node's marker instead of refusing; place a new proof in its statement's file), `assets/ai/rules.md` (allow quoting `\end{ENV}` for `--after`, or add `--after-block`). Rules: an hour; adopt: a day.

**EXP-5. The evidence for a claim cannot be opened from the viewer, and the session's "What it did" is empty.** Weight: hurts. Recommendation: fix. Rule 7 puts a trial's script in `.loom/sessions/<id>/`, but every path with a segment starting with `.` is hidden (`loom/src/loom/files.py:3`, `Shown.is_shown`), so `loom link` refuses it and the transcript can only name `vertices.py` as code text. Fix: list and serve a session directory's files read-only on its page, or tell agents to keep evidence in a shown folder; log shell commands to the active session. Hours each.

**EXP-6. Dead ends, claims and asides are all "Note"; a claim never counts as open.** Weight: hurts. Recommendation: keep the types mechanism, ship two defaults, fix rule 10. The kind lives only in the first words of the message; Notes lists them in reading order beside older asides (shot 19); a note on a node no document reaches (`sh-0402`) is in no Notes list, only reachable by a link; and since Note only informs, an unchecked claim is in neither `loom status` nor To do. What already works: the person made a "Dead end" type (static, listed in Notes, with agent instructions) in one dialog, Settings › Annotations › Add type (shots 54-55); it was written to `.loom/annotation-types.toml` and appeared in `loom ai orient` at once as `--type dead-end`. But rule 10 still told the agent to use Note, so it received two instructions. Separately, `loom ai annotations --status open` lists Note annotations as open (as QF-B9).

**EXP-7. No single place says what is open.** Weight: hurts. Recommendation: fix by convention (below) plus EXP-1. Pieces exist and disagree in scope: the research state's Open list (only as good as the agent's last write; the person edits it in their own editor, not the viewer), `loom status` (agents only; incomplete keys and open annotations, no notes), Review › To do (open types only, all reviews mixed, raw TeX in titles), Never accepted (folded, raw reason), ⌘K "incomplete" (found the one incomplete node). The research state worked best: rendered, linked, every link opening the thing.

("Theorem ??", "Proposition ??") although the banner says they "use context saved 2026-11-13" (shot 14); its lemma is numbered 1.1, which also names it in the copy review ("Lemma 1.1 · What is left") and in transcript links ("Lemma 1.1"). ## The least that makes exploring workable in v0.1, without a problem page

Each is a convention existing pieces carry; sizes are honest guesses.

1. **The question is a Question annotation on the statement it concerns, and one entry in the research state.** The person asks in the viewer, or the agent writes a {type:question}; the agent keeps an Open/Closed list in the research state, one entry per question, linking the question annotation, the session and the dead ends. Change: the research-state paragraph and the "problem opened or closed" row in `assets/ai/rules.md`, a line in `orientation.md` §2. An hour. Rests on what works today.
2. **What is open is drawn where it is read.** EXP-1's margin mark and reason; optionally the research state page also shows a computed list of incomplete keys and open Question/Claim annotations under the agent's text (computed from the manifest, no second store, P3). Files: `assets/loom.sty`, `DocumentItem.svelte`/`FragmentNotes.svelte`, the research state route. Hours for the mark; a day with the computed list.
3. **Dead ends and claims are annotation types.** Ship "Dead end" (static, listed in Notes) and "Claim" (open until resolved, so it counts in `loom status` and To do and never reads as proved; the message states how far it got and names its evidence; the person resolves it when it becomes a statement or is dropped) in `assets/init/annotation-types.toml`, written by `loom upgrade` where missing; rule 10 names them by placeholder. A dead end goes on the question, and on the step that misled if that is elsewhere, linking the question. Hours.
4. **Before an attempt, read what was tried.** Rules 9 and the question and draft modes: read the research state entry and `loom ai annotations` on the question and its neighbours, `--json` for the messages; add `--around KEY` when there is a day for it (EXP-2).
5. **Attempts live in a section copy; a finished single result arrives as a suggested edit.** Each attempt a titled `proof` (`[Attempt N: idea]`) whose failure is an `\incomplete` with the reason (readable once item 2 lands); a proof that works is filed as suggested edits on the author's key. Change: `rules.md` allows quoting `\end{ENV}` with `--after`, or `cli/suggest.py` gains a placement after the block; `adopt.py` stops naming commands (EXP-4). Hours for the rule, a day for adopt.
6. **Evidence is somewhere the viewer shows.** The session page lists its directory's files read-only, or agents keep scripts in a shown folder; shell commands log to the active session (EXP-5). Hours.
7. **Fleeting capture keeps every key.** EXP-3's fix, and an "only mine" filter in Comments. Hours.

Watch: how many live questions each pilot has at once; whether the research-state entries stay current without being asked; whether the person opens the research state or goes straight to the paper; whether an agent repeats a recorded dead end.
