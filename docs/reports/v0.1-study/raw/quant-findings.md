**The five that would most hurt a pilot learning from books and papers:**

1. **A lesson is outside review.** Neither the person nor an agent can annotate or suggest an edit to a Markdown file. The learner's main documents get none of loom's review loop, and the agent has to edit the file in place.
2. **No undo, and no news of what the person changed.** Unless `track_edits` is on, an agent's rewrite of a lesson or outline has nothing behind it (item 36), and `since` never tells the agent which files the person changed (item 3). This touches the "never damages a person's files" bar.
3. **Loom speaks to the learner in LaTeX terms.** It says "nothing is being worked on" and "no keys of yours yet", and shows 57 errors on exercise answers, while the orientation tells the agent to plan from status (items 4, 8, 14).
4. **Dollar amounts eat the text.** Any lesson in finance or economics loses words between two prices (item 19).
5. **Links in lessons rot silently.** Heading slugs break on a rename, `cited:work:` ids change when a book's details are corrected, and nothing checks either (items 13, 22, 37).

Close behind: scanned books with no text and wrong section labels (6, 24, 33), and citekey names in tabs (23).
