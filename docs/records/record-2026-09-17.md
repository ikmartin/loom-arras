## What was done

### WQ-04: identity candidates (loom)

- `loom/src/loom/refs/resolve.py`: a `Query` (from a bibliography entry, or later from a `\bibitem` string), parsers for zbMATH Open's `_search` and Crossref's `query.bibliographic`, loom's own scoring, pooling of one work's records into one candidate, spacing of a second between requests to each service, a response cache, and the candidate record in the work's own `refs/work/<hash>/resolved.json`.
- `loom refs resolve [CITEKEY...] [--refresh] [--json]`, refused without `[refs] resolve = true`; `[refs] contact` optional.
- `loom:unresolved-work` names a recorded candidate and the field to add, reading only the record.
- The manifest's references gain an additive `candidates` list; arras shows the first as an unconfirmed dashed link.
- Terms checked 2026-09-17: zbMATH Open needs no key, licenses its data CC-BY-SA 4.0, asks for a reasonable rate; Crossref needs no key and reported `x-rate-limit-limit: 1` per second on the public pool.

## Design changes

**Candidates, not identities.** WQ-04 planned resolved identifiers that bind with provenance `resolved` and are offered as a diff to `refs.bib`. The author had ruled the bibliography out of loom's scope the day before, and where a confirmed identity is recorded is an open WQ-23 question, so nothing binds. WQ-02's trigger was rewritten accordingly (DR-122).

**Loom scores, not the services.** Crossref ranked two chapters of Silverman's book above the book itself, and the two services' scores are on unrelated scales. Scoring by title, first author and year in loom picked the book and makes a confidence mean one thing.

**Records of one work are pooled.** zbMATH gave Silverman's Zbl number and Crossref the book's DOI, at equal confidence, first as two candidates. They are now one candidate led by the DOI.

**zbMATH answers 404 for no match, and biblatex writes `date`.** Both surfaced only against the live services: the Stacks project reported a failure instead of no match, and relloc's biblatex entries scored 0.90 instead of 1.00 until `date` was read as the year.

**A digest's sections are not external.** The work graph first left three digest section nodes uncontracted; they carry `external: false` and no `digest`, and are recognised by their file.

**The full work-graph quotient waits.** Contracting the corpus's own results as well gives one node and a star for a single corpus, so only external nodes are contracted (DR-124).

**The fixture's cause dates followed the day it was regenerated.** Refreshing the conformance fixture on 2026-09-17 moved five `acceptance.causes[].when` values from 2026-09-16, because they come from file modification times, which `LOOM_FIXED_TIME` did not override; the fixture test passed only because every earlier run fell on the same day. `_when` in `loom/src/loom/records/store.py` now returns the fixed date under `LOOM_FIXED_TIME`, as chapter 12.10 says the variable is for, and the refreshed fixture differs from the old one only by the reworded `loom:unresolved-work` message.

**Chapter 12's reference had drifted.** §12.2 says it is the generated reference, but nothing regenerates it: it had no `loom refs` section and gave digest paths under `refs/`. Its `loom digest` and `loom refs` sections were replaced from `loom/docs/cli-reference.md`; the rest of the chapter was not re-synced, and the generator's `ai promote` help still says a digest is copied "to refs/".
