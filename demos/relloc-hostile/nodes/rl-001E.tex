\begin{definition}\label{rl-001E}\label{def:stabilizer-fixed-stack}
  Let \(G = T\) be a split torus of rank \(r\) over \(S\) acting on a quasi-DM stack \(X\) over \(S\). Denote by \(\mathcal X\) the quotient stack \([X/T]\) as before. We define \(X^{sT}\) as the stack over \(X\) whose \(A\)-valued points, for an \(X\)-scheme \(x:A\to X\), are the closed subgroups of \(\Aut_{\mathcal X}(x)\) which are affine and smooth over \(A\) with connected fibers of dimension \(r\).
\end{definition}
