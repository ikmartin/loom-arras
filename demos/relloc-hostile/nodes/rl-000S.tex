\subsection{Euler classes of vector bundle stacks}\label{rl-000S}\label{sec:euler-classes-of-vector-bundle-stacks}

In this section we denote vector bundle stacks with ordinary letters \(V\) rather than fraktur letters for readability.

\bigskip

\noindent\textbf{Convention (the localized group.)} Let \(T\) be a split diagonalizable group, \(R_T = A^*_T(\pt)\). For an algebraic stack \(\mathcal X\) with a trivial \(T\) action, let \(S\) denote the multiplicative set of operators on \(A^T_*(\mathcal X)\) generated by the equivariant Euler classes \(e_T(V)\) of finite rank moving \(T\)-equivariant vector bundles \(V\) on \(\mathcal X\). We then define
\begin{align*}
  A_T^*(\mathcal X)_{\loc} = S^{-1}A^T_*(\mathcal X).
\end{align*}
For \(T = \mathbb G_m\) this localization maps to the ring
\begin{align*}
  A_*(\mathcal X)(\!(t^{-1})\!) = A_*(\mathcal X)[\![t]\!][t]
\end{align*}
of formal Laurent series in \(t^{-1}\) by expanding
\begin{align*}
  e_T(L)^{-1} = (wt + c)^{-1} = \sum_{k\geq 0}(-1)^kc^k(wt)^{-k-1}.
\end{align*}
