\begin{proof}[Second proof of Proposition~\ref{sh-0007}]\label{sh-0010}
\uses{sh-0004}
The quotient $W(\quiv) / \Zcyc(\quiv)$ embeds in $\ZZ^{V}$ by the first isomorphism theorem, hence is torsion-free, and a sublattice with torsion-free quotient is saturated. The cycle lattice is the kernel of Definition~\ref{sh-0004}, so nothing else is needed.
\end{proof}
