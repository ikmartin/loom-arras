Theorem (Rank)

Let \(\quiv\) be a quiver whose underlying graph has \(c\) connected components. Then

\[\rank \Zcyc(\quiv) \;=\; |A(\quiv)| - |V(\quiv)| + c.\]
Proof

The image of \(\bd\) is the set of integer vectors summing to zero on each component, a sublattice of \(\ZZ^{V}\) of rank \(|V(\quiv)| - c\); this is [Arden24, Proposition 2.1]. Since \(\Zcyc(\quiv) = \ker \bd\) is saturated by Proposition sh-0007, the rank of the kernel is the rank of the source less the rank of the image, which gives (eq:rank).
