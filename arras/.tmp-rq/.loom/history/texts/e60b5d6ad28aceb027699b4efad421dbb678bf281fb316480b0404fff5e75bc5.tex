\begin{proof}
\uses{sh-0007}
The image of $\bd$ is the set of integer vectors summing to zero on each component, a sublattice of $\ZZ^{V}$ of rank $|V(\quiv)| - c$; this is \cite[Proposition 2.1]{Arden24}. Since $\Zcyc(\quiv) = \ker \bd$ is saturated by Proposition~\ref{sh-0007}, the rank of the kernel is the rank of the source less the rank of the image, which gives \eqref{eq:rank}.
