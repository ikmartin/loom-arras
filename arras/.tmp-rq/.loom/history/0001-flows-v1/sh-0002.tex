\begin{definition}[Quiver]\label{sh-0002}
A \emph{quiver} $\quiv$ is a quadruple $(V, A, s, t)$ with $V$ and $A$ finite sets and $s, t \colon A \to V$ the source and target maps. A \emph{weighting} of $\quiv$ is a function $w \colon A \to \ZZ$, and the weightings form a lattice $W(\quiv) \cong \ZZ^{A}$ under pointwise addition.
\end{definition}
