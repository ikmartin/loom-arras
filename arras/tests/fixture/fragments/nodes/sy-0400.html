Definition 1 (Gadget)

\label{sy-0008}\label{def:gadget}\label{defn:gadget-old}
A \emph{gadget} is a widget whose involution has no fixed point.

Lemma 3 (Orbits)

\label{sy-0002}
Every orbit of a widget has one or two points; compare \eqref{eq:fix} in Definition~\ref{sy-0001}.
Proof
The orbit of $x$ is $\{x, \sigma x\}$ because
\begin{equation}\label{eq:step}
\sigma(\sigma x) = x
\end{equation}
by \eqref{eq:prose}.
