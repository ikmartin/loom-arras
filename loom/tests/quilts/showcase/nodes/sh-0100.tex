\section{Introduction}\label{sh-0100}

A quiver carries two lattices: the weightings of its arrows, and the balanced weightings, those that neither create nor destroy anything at a vertex. This paper is about the second. Our main result, Theorem~\ref{sh-0009}, computes its rank from the combinatorics of the underlying graph alone, and Theorem~\ref{sh-000C} counts the balanced weightings of bounded height.

The cycle lattice of a quiver is classical, and we follow \cite[Proposition 2.1]{Arden24} throughout; the counting argument of Section~\ref{sh-0300} is an adaptation of the median-order technique of \cite[Theorem 2.3]{Bellamy19}. We also use the exchange bound of \cite[Lemma 5.9]{Arden24}, which the digest of that paper does not carry. The survey \cite{Cortez07} lists what is known for infinite quivers, none of which we use.
