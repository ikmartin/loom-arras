\begin{remark}[{\cite[Remark 5.3, p.~3]{Arden24A}}]\label{Arden24A-rem-5.3}
\label{Arden24A-rem:local}
The converse of Corollary~\ref{Arden24A-cor-5.2} fails as soon as the support contains a cycle: the weighting that is $1$ on every arrow of a directed cycle and $0$ elsewhere is balanced and nonzero. What the corollary rules out is a balanced weighting whose support is acyclic, and that is the form in which Theorem~\ref{Arden24A-thm-3.1} is usually applied.
\end{remark}
