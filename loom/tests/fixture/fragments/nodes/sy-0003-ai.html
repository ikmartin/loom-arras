Theorem 2.1 (Parity)

Let \((X,\sigma)\) be a finite widget. Then \(|X| \equiv |\Fix(\sigma)| \pmod 2\).

First proof

By Lemma  1.3 the set \(X\) is a disjoint union of orbits of size one or two. □

Second proof

Count fixed points of the permutation \(\sigma\) directly, using (3) from the proof of Lemma  1.3 and Definition  1.1. □
