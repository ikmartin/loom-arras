Lemma 2.3 (Deferred)

The composite of two widget maps is a widget map.

Definition 2.4 (Gadget)

A gadget is a widget whose involution has no fixed point.

Proof of Lemma  2.3

Both maps commute with the involutions, so their composite does. Here we use Definition  2.4 only for notation. □
